# Architecture decision record

> Record one decision per file. Name each file with a number and a short title, for example ADR-007-use-a-pam-gateway.md, and never delete a record: supersede it with a new one.

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Title | A short phrase that states the decision |
| Status | Proposed, accepted, deprecated, or superseded by another record |
| Date | The date of the decision |
| Deciders | Who made the decision |
| Related | The patterns, requirements, and threats that this decision addresses |

## Context

Describe the problem, the forces at play, and the constraints. Include the requirements and threats that make a decision necessary.

## Options considered

| Option | Advantages | Disadvantages |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The first option | What it does well | What it costs, or the risk it adds |
| The second option | What it does well | What it costs, or the risk it adds |

## Decision

State the decision in one or two sentences, starting with a verb such as "adopt" or "require".

## Consequences

Describe what becomes easier and what becomes harder, the new risks that the decision creates, and what must happen next.

## Review

State when this decision should be reviewed, or the event that would make it wrong.
